# Football axis benchmarks: research pass (6 Nov 2026)

The owner asked for "realistic benchmarks for numbers", so that walking 18–20k steps
already counts as elite and nobody has to go past 20k for a small gain. This pass checks
each row of spec §6.2 against published ranges. A row changes only where a source
contradicts it.

| Axis | Spec row | Source | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pace (steps) | 0/2k/4k/6k/8k/10k/12k/15k/18k/20k+ → 1/3/5/8/10/12/14/16/19/20 | Tudor-Locke & Bassett, "How many steps/day are enough?" (2004) and Tudor-Locke et al. (2011): under 5,000 sedentary, 5,000–7,499 low active, 7,500–9,999 somewhat active, 10,000+ active, 12,500+ highly active. Paluch et al., Lancet Public Health (2022): mortality benefit levels off around 8,000–10,000 a day for adults under 60. | Keep. 10k lands at 12 (the 11–15 "good" band), 12.5k at about 14 ("highly active"), and the top band (16–20) runs 15k to 20k, so 18–20k is elite and the scale stops at 20k. |
| Rest (sleep) | <5/5/6/7/7.5/8/8.5+ h → 4/6/9/13/15/17/18, rested rating ±2 | AASM and Sleep Research Society consensus (Watson et al., 2015): adults need 7 or more hours. National Sleep Foundation (Hirshkowitz et al., 2015): 7–9 h recommended, 6 h and 10 h "may be appropriate", under 6 not recommended. | Keep. 7 h is the first "good" value (13); under 6 h falls in the 6–10 band; 8.5 h and up does not climb further, so more sleep is not pushed past 9 h. |
| Fuel (food) | inside the calorie range 1.0, ≤10% over 0.6, under the floor 0.4, >10% over 0.2; protein share of target | No body numbers are used: the range and the protein target are the owner's own settings. Under the floor scoring below inside the range keeps the rule that eating less is never rewarded. | Keep. |
| Power (training) | done 16, extra 18, short 11, kept rest day 13, missed 4 | Follows the owner's own plan (sessions per week in settings); no outside benchmark applies. | Keep. |
| Routine (checklist) | 0/50/80/100% → 3/10/15/19 | Share of the owner's own checklist; no outside benchmark applies. | Keep. |
| Shape (body logs) | 6, +1.5 a weigh-in in 7 days (up to 5), +3 waist, +2 trend toward goal | Rewards logging and direction, never a body number. | Keep. |

No row changed, so there are no rulings to record.
