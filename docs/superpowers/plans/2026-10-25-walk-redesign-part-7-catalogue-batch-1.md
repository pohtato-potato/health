# Walk redesign, part 7, batch 1: the Indian history challenges — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The nine Indian history challenges written, sourced, fact-checked and on the shelf: Shivaji's escape, the Dandi March, the kos minars, the Rani's last campaign, the night ride from Meerut, the Maratha forts, the Deccan sultanates, the Indus cities, the spice coast.

**Architecture:** Content only, in the shape part 1 fixed. Each route is one file in `src/walk/catalogue/routes/<id>.ts` with its waypoints (longitude, latitude) in `scripts/geo/routes.json`, regenerated by `scripts/make-geo.py` into `src/walk/geo/routes.ts`. Four routes are new files (`rani`, `forts`, `indus`, `spice`), registered in `src/walk/catalogue/index.ts` in shelf order. A stricter test for ready routes comes first, so no route reaches the shelf half-written.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript data, Python generator, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-walk-redesign-design.md` ("How history is told", "The catalogue", "Data"). Briefs: `Walk shared/from-claude-code/2026-10-23-challenges-deep-dive-part-2.md`, routes 1–9.

## Global Constraints

- "Every route ships with its line, its places, its signature and its written pages, each fact with a source. A route without its writing done does not appear on the shelf."
- "Then vs you only where a record exists, tagged by kind of record… Where there's no record, the app says nothing rather than invent."
- "All sides, critically: conquest, colonialism, state myth and Partition are told plainly and from every side." Tradition is labelled as tradition; disputed numbers are given as ranges or hedged.
- Religious places as a mix, never the focus; no deity framing (owner's rule).
- Written in our own words: no quotations.
- Each route's ink is its own and never gold.
- Pages: a line, a page of two or three short paragraphs, and a "go deeper" on the brief's themes where it earns one. Sentences short enough for a low-vision reader.
- Challenges up to 1,500 km.

## Review Focus

1. **Wrong or over-firm facts** on contested history (1857's names, Shivaji's escape, Talikota, the Indus script, da Gama's violence): each told with its uncertainty. Fresh fact-check, Task 3.
2. **A day with no page:** a ready route whose first page starts after km 0, or with long gaps, leaves the route page empty or stale. Test in Task 1.
3. **Places that don't climb the line** (wrong `at`, km out of step): the dot jumps. `lineProblems` for every ready route, Task 1.
4. **Long names** on cards and visas at 360 px ("Shivaji's escape from Agra", "The night ride from Meerut"). Look in Task 3.
5. **Then and you** on a route without a day record: must not appear. Existing catalogue test, plus a look at each route page.

### Task 1: Readiness test (`tests/walk-catalogue.test.ts`)

For every route with `ready: true`:
- `lineProblems(def, ROUTE_LINES[def.id])` is empty;
- the first page is at km 0, and no two pages are more than `max(60, km / 8)` km apart;
- every page has a line and at least one paragraph;
- a signature exists, with at least six marks;
- `then` (if any) names only places on the route;
- at least two sources.
Run: fails for nothing yet (only Xuanzang is ready, and it must pass); then each route below must pass it before `ready: true`.
